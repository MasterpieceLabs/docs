Quick answers to the questions borrowers ask most often. If your question is not here, reach out to your Masterpiece Labs point of contact.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit my invoice after submitting a loan request?">
    Yes, until your application reaches `Approved` or `Rejected`. Note that you cannot reduce invoice size below the 70% ceiling of your current requested advance; lower the loan request first if you need to shrink the invoice.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change my requested amount after approval?">
    No. Once your application is `Approved` or `Rejected`, borrower edits to the loan request and invoice are locked.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which buyers does Masterpiece Labs support?">
    Masterpiece Labs maintains an internal list of allowed buyers. If your invoice is from a buyer not on that list, the application will prompt you to include additional supporting documentation about the buyer's financials, which we use in underwriting.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I have to sign the loan agreement before setting up DACA?">
    No. You can start DACA setup before signing the loan agreement.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do I complete business verification?">
    Inside the Masterpiece Labs app. The verification button opens a hosted Didit session at `verify.masterpiecelabs.xyz`. Masterpiece Labs receives the decision through a signed webhook and updates your status.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if my buyer pays early?">
    Masterpiece Labs settles your loan as soon as the payment clears the DACA account. You do not need to wait for the end of the term.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
