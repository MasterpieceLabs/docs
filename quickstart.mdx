title: "Get Approved for Financing in Six Steps"
sidebarTitle: "Quickstart"
description: "Sign in, set up your business, add an invoice, request an advance, upload documents, and complete verification to get a Masterpiece Labs decision."
---

This quickstart walks you from account creation to a submitted, verified application. Most borrowers can finish in a single sitting once documents are ready.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in with your work email">
    Log in through Auth0 with a verified work email. You will be prompted to verify the address if you have not already.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your business profile and bank account">
    Enter your legal business name, ABA routing number, and bank account number. Masterpiece Labs will use this account to fund your advance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your buyer invoice">
    Add the buyer, invoice size, and payment terms for the invoice you want to finance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request an advance">
    Choose an amount up to 70% of the invoice and a term of 1, 2, or 3 months. The calculator shows your rate live as you type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload supporting documents">
    Upload the documents the app requests. Each file is scanned before it counts as committed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete business verification">
    Finish the Didit KYB flow in-app to verify your business identity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Masterpiece Labs reviews your application and returns a decision with an approved amount and monthly rate.

<Tip>
  Once you are approved, you continue with loan agreement signing, DACA setup, and proof of remittance. See [What Happens After Approval](/after-approval/overview).
</Tip>
