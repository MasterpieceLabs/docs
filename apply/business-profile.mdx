---
title: "Set Up Your Business Profile and Bank Account"
sidebarTitle: "Business Profile"
description: "Enter your legal business name and bank details so Masterpiece Labs can fund advances and debit repayments from the right account."
---

Your business profile tells Masterpiece Labs who you are and where to move money. You cannot add an invoice or request an advance until it is complete.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in with a verified email">
    Log in through Auth0. If your email is not verified yet, the app will prompt you before letting you continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your legal business name">
    Use the exact name on your formation documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your bank details">
    Provide your ABA routing number and bank account number. Only the last four digits of the account number are shown back to you in the app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A verified email is required to create or edit your business profile.
</Note>

<Warning>
  The profile must be complete before you can add an invoice or submit a loan request.
</Warning>

## Editing your profile later

You can edit your business profile at any time. On later edits you may leave the bank account number blank to keep the stored value unchanged; other fields you leave blank keep their existing values as well.
