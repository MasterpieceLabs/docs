Masterpiece Labs turns an unpaid invoice from a frontier lab into working capital today. Submit the invoice, get an advance of up to 70% of its value, and repay from the lab's payment once it lands, all inside a bank account that Masterpiece Labs controls until you are made whole.

This site walks borrowers through applying, getting approved, and closing out a loan.

## Who Masterpiece Labs is for

You are a good fit for Masterpiece Labs if you sell data or RL environments to frontier labs such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or xAI, wait 30 to 90 days for payment, and want cash to keep operating in the meantime.

<Note>
  Masterpiece Labs is invoice financing, not equity, not a factoring buy-out. You keep the customer relationship; the advance is a short-term loan repaid from that specific invoice.
</Note>
