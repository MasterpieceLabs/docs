## Order and dependencies

<Note>
  You can start DACA setup before signing the loan agreement. But the Notice of Exclusive Control step opens only once DACA setup has moved past `Not Opened`.
</Note>

A typical order is:

1. Sign the loan agreement.
2. Complete Slash onboarding and mark DACA setup as user-complete.
3. Wait for Masterpiece Labs to countersign and mark DACA complete.
4. Sign the Notice of Exclusive Control.
5. Upload proof of remittance once your buyer pays.

<Info>
  Once all four steps are done, your application moves into post-acceptance status, which shows the outstanding balance and any remittance activity.
</Info>
