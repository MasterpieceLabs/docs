Proof of remittance is the final borrower step. When your buyer pays the invoice into the Slash DACA account, upload proof of the payment so Masterpiece Labs can settle the loan.

## What to upload

Upload the bank statement, remittance advice, or wire confirmation that shows the incoming payment. The app will list the accepted document types.

## How the upload works

Uploads follow the same flow as your supporting documents: a direct upload to Masterpiece Labs's private storage using a presigned URL, followed by a virus scan before the file counts as committed.

<Warning>
  Wait for the scan to complete before you close the tab. The step is not finished until the scan passes.
</Warning>

## After you upload

Masterpiece Labs reconciles the incoming payment against your loan balance:

- Principal and interest are repaid to Masterpiece Labs first.
- Any excess is released to you.

Your dashboard then shows post-acceptance status with the outstanding balance, remittance activity, and confirmation once the loan is fully settled.

<Info>
  If your buyer pays before the end of the term, Masterpiece Labs settles as soon as the payment clears the DACA account.
</Info>
