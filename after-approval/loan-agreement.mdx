Your loan agreement locks in the approved amount, term, and monthly rate. You sign it inside the Masterpiece Labs app, in an embedded Docusign session.

## Signing the agreement

The app opens a Docusign Focused View pre-filled with your approved terms. Read the agreement, sign, and finish inside the embedded view. You do not need a Docusign account.

## When the step is complete

The browser sees a `signing_complete` event as soon as you finish signing, but Masterpiece Labs marks the step complete only when Docusign confirms completion server-side. That confirmation usually arrives within seconds. You will see a "waiting for secure confirmation" state until it does.

<Info>
  Docusign Connect is the source of truth for completion. The finalization flow will not advance past this step on browser confirmation alone.
</Info>

<Warning>
  If a Docusign envelope is declined or voided, you cannot re-sign the same agreement type. Contact your Masterpiece Labs point of contact if you need a new envelope.
</Warning>

## After signing

Once the loan agreement is `COMPLETED`, the app shows the fully executed document and unblocks any steps waiting on it. You can proceed with DACA setup in parallel or continue in order.
