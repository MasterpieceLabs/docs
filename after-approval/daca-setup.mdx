DACA setup opens the bank account your buyer will pay into. The Deposit Account Control Agreement gives Masterpiece Labs priority claim on that account so your advance is repaid first from any incoming funds.

## What you do

1. Follow the Masterpiece Labs app's link to Slash onboarding.
2. Complete Slash's account setup steps for your business.
3. Return to the Masterpiece Labs app and mark DACA setup as user-complete.
4. Masterpiece Labs reviews your setup and marks it complete.

## Statuses you will see

- **Not Opened.** Default. You have not started Slash onboarding.
- **User Complete.** You finished onboarding on your side and marked the step complete.
- **Complete.** Masterpiece Labs has confirmed your setup after review.

<Info>
  Masterpiece Labs can return a completed setup to `User Complete` if a follow-up correction is needed, without reopening it all the way to `Not Opened`.
</Info>

<Note>
  You can start DACA setup before signing the loan agreement. The finalization flow does not require the loan agreement to be signed first.
</Note>

<Warning>
  The Notice of Exclusive Control step opens only once your DACA setup has moved past `Not Opened`. If you plan to sign both in one sitting, complete Slash onboarding first.
</Warning>
